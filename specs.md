## 01 Chevrolet Corvette Z06 (C8), 2023
- 5.5L LT6 naturally aspirated flat-plane V8, 670 hp. 0–60 2.6 s is Chevrolet's claim with the Z07 package (secondary, via GM Authority).
- Top speed: launch material said 186 mph; chief engineer Tadge Juechter quoted ~195 mph without Z07 and ~189 with it (not a formal spec); Hennessey measured 188. Poster keeps 189 so the top speed and the 2.6 s time describe the same Z07 car.
- Paint: Accelerate Yellow Metallic, GD0, offered 2020–2024 (dropped for 2025). Not a "Tintcoat".
- Clue: the Z06 body is 3.6 in wider than a Stingray, with enlarged side intakes behind the doors feeding engine cooling air.
- Logo: the C8 crossed flags (see `logos/SOURCES.md`).
- Sources: https://gmauthority.com/blog/2021/10/2023-corvette-z06-horsepower-torque-figures-revealed/ ; https://www.musclecarsandtrucks.com/2023-c8-corvette-z06-top-speed-195-mph-tadge-jeuchter-josh-holder/ ; https://www.corvsport.com/2024-corvette-z06-in-depth-guide/ ; https://gmauthority.com/blog/2024/10/here-are-all-the-2025-corvette-colors/amp

## 02 Ford Mustang Dark Horse (S650), 2024
- 5.0L Coyote V8, 500 hp.
- 0–60: no Ford claim in mph. MotorTrend (via Ford Authority) and Road & Track both tested 4.1 s; a later MotorTrend run did 3.7 s; Edmunds 4.6. Poster: 4.1 s (two independent tests agree).
- Top speed: Ford EU technical spec sheet states 263 km/h = 163 mph (primary). A 166 mph US figure circulates without a primary source; Hennessey measured 161. Poster: 163.
- Paint: Blue Ember Metallic is listed on Ford's own Dark Horse page. Paint code not found.
- Clue correction: the tri-bar taillights are on every S650 Mustang, not just the Dark Horse. Fine for a kid (it spots "a Mustang"), but it is not Dark Horse-specific.
- Sources: https://media.ford.com/content/dam/fordmedia/Europe/en/2024/01/NewMustang/2024_Ford_Mustang_technical_specification_EU.pdf ; https://www.ford.com/cars/mustang/2026/models/dark-horse ; https://fordauthority.com/2023/11/2024-ford-mustang-dark-horse-beats-bmw-m2-in-new-comparison ; https://www.motorbiscuit.com/just-how-fast-is-the-2024-ford-mustang-dark-horse

## 03 Dodge Charger SRT Hellcat Widebody (LD), 2023
- 6.2L supercharged HEMI V8, 717 hp.
- 0–60 3.6 s: Dodge figure as quoted by Car and Driver via SlashGear (secondary). C&D tested a non-widebody Hellcat at 3.8 s.
- Top speed 196 mph (Widebody); 203 mph is the 797 hp Redeye. Some outlets wrongly give 203 for the plain Widebody.
- Year set to 2023: Stellantis' 2023 fact sheet lists Plum Crazy (PHG) for the Charger. 2021–22 availability not verified.
- Clue: Stellantis calls it the "signature LED racetrack tail lamp".
- Sources: https://media.stellantisnorthamerica.com/newsrelease.do?id=24153 ; https://media.stellantisnorthamerica.com/newsrelease.do?id=16137 ; https://www.slashgear.com/1210946/heres-how-fast-the-dodge-charger-srt-hellcat-really-is ; https://www.motor1.com/news/431834/2021-dodge-charger-srt-hellcat-redeye/

## 04 Porsche 911 Turbo S (992.1), 2021
- 3.7L twin-turbo flat-six, 640 hp (650 PS). 0–60 2.6 s and top track speed 205 mph, both from Porsche Cars North America's Turbo S press kit (primary).
- Clue confirmed in the same press kit: "a light bar that is characteristic of the current 911 generation" at the rear, and LED Matrix headlights. The round headlights are a 911 signature.
- Paint: Carrara White Metallic is a long-standing 911 colour; not checked against a 992 Turbo S configurator (open item).
- Logo: the Porsche crest (see `logos/SOURCES.md`).
- Sources: https://newsroom.porsche.com/dam/jcr:c009aaf4-be47-434f-99f6-cbe0ee488950/PCNA%20Press%20Kit_%20Type%20992%20911%20Turbo%20S%20Coupe%20and%20Cabriolet.pdf ; https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/2020/products/porsche-new-911-turbo-s-coupe-cabriolet-top-of-the-range-premiere-20110.html

## 05 Lamborghini Murciélago LP640, 2006
- 6.5L V12, 640 PS = 631 hp. 0–62 mph 3.4 s (Lamborghini claim, 0–100 km/h; no 0–60 claim found). Top speed 340 km/h = 211 mph (coupe; roadster 205).
- Paint: Verde Ithaca, code 0077 pearl. LP640s were delivered in it (dealer and auction listings), possibly as special-order paint (secondary).
- Clue correction: the shoulder scoops (VACS) above the rear wheels open by themselves when the engine needs more cooling air, not simply at a set speed, so the note says "pop open" without "at speed". They are on both sides and asymmetric (left oil cooler, right radiator).
- Logo: the Lamborghini bull shield (see `logos/SOURCES.md`).
- Sources: https://supercarnostalgia.com/blog/lamborghini-murcielago-lp640 ; https://www.lambocars.com/murcielago-lp640-specs-performance/ ; https://www.carwhere.com/car-colors/lamborghini/verde-ithaca ; https://www.ferrarichat.com/forum/posts/147076245

## 06 Ferrari 296 GTB, 2022
- 2,992 cc 120° twin-turbo V6 plus electric motor, 830 cv combined = 819 hp. 0–100 km/h 2.9 s, top speed 330 km/h = 205 mph (Ferrari claims).
- Clue verified on the reference photo and in launch coverage: a single central exhaust tailpipe at the top of the rear bumper between the tail lights.
- Paint: Rosso Corsa, Ferrari's standard red. Not checked on the 296 configurator.
- Sources: https://corporate.ferrari.com/en/296-gtb-defining-fun-drive ; https://www.supercars.net/blog/2022-ferrari-296-gtb-unveiled ; https://www.carwale.com/news/ferrari-296-gtb-globally-unveiled/
