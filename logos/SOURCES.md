# Logo sources

Private home prints only. All marks are trademarks of their owners. Every file is the owner's
artwork as published on the site below; `prepare_logos.py` only renders SVG, removes a plain white
backdrop, crops, and keeps or drops whole connected shapes. Nothing is drawn, traced or recoloured.
Originals are in `src/`; the poster-ready PNGs are `<name>.png`.
